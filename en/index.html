About

"An engineer who understands business — and a creative who can build"

I'm Nicola. I live at the foot of the Alps, near Turin. I lead a team of 30 people at a tech company — and I also work independently, for people who need to turn an idea into something real.

In 15 years I've walked the whole path: from writing code to setting strategy. That lets me talk with you and with the engineering team without anything getting lost in between.

Outside work I play bass on live stages and hike in the Alps. I believe the best ideas come when you stop and listen.
